About us

We help families take better care of the pets they love.

Pets cannot tell us when something is wrong. CuteFamilyPet makes it easier to notice, keep track and act at the right time.

Our story

Most pet owners keep health details scattered across vet invoices, pill bottles, sticky notes and phone photos. When a pet suddenly seems unwell, it is hard to remember when the last dose was given, whether vaccines are current, or whether that limp started last week or last month.

CuteFamilyPet started with a simple idea: put everything about a pet's health in one friendly place that the whole household can share, and give owners clear, careful guidance about when a symptom needs a vet.

Our mission

To help every pet family spot problems earlier, never miss a medication or vaccine, and walk into the vet's office with the full picture.

🛟

Safety first

Our symptom checker is built to err on the side of caution. Emergency signs always come first, and AI can only add caution, never remove it.

🤝

Vets are partners

We support your vet, not replace them. Records, photos and symptom history are easy to share before an appointment.

🔒

Your data is yours

Start without an account. When you sync, your records are private to you and the people you invite, and you can export or delete them any time.
