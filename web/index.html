How it works

Up and running in two minutes

  1. Add your pets

    Name, species, breed, birthday and weight. Add a photo while you are at it.

  2. Log what matters

    Meds, vaccines, weigh-ins and symptoms. Reminders take care of the rest.

  3. Check and share

    Run a symptom check when something seems off, and share records, photos or videos with your vet.

AI symptom checker

A second pair of eyes when something looks wrong

Describe the symptoms, add a photo, and CuteFamilyPet gives you an urgency level with plain-language reasons and what to watch for next.

  • Safety rules run first and catch emergencies instantly, even offline.
  • The AI can raise the urgency based on the photo, but never lower it.
  • Your photo is used only for that check and is not used to train AI models.

CuteFamilyPet gives general guidance, not a diagnosis. If you are worried, call your vet or an emergency animal hospital.
