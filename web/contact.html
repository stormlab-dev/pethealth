FAQ

Common questions

Is CuteFamilyPet free?

Yes. The free plan covers pet profiles, health logs, reminders, the rule-based symptom checker and up to 200 photos and short videos. Premium and Family add unlimited media, AI photo checks, vet-ready reports and more.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Google Play or the App Store. Cancel any time in your phone's subscription settings, and you keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

Does the symptom checker replace my vet?

No. It gives general guidance about urgency so you can decide what to do next. It cannot examine your pet or make a diagnosis. If you are worried, call your vet.

What happens to the photos I use for an AI check?

The photo is sent securely to our AI service for that one check and the result comes back to your app. It is not used to train AI models.

Can I share my pet's records with family or a pet sitter?

Yes. Once you sign in to sync, invite people as a caregiver (can log doses and symptoms) or viewer (read only).

Which vet clinics and pharmacies are supported?

We are adding partners now. If you would like your clinic or pharmacy connected, tell us below.

When is the iPhone app coming?

It is in testing now. Choose "iPhone beta" in the form to be invited to TestFlight.
