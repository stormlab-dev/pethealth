Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

Draft for review. This policy describes how the app is built today. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before public launch.

What we collect

Without an account, your pets' records, photos and videos stay on your device or in your browser. We do not receive them.

With sync turned on, we store your email address and the records you add (pets, symptoms, medications, vaccines, weights, appointments, photos and videos) so they are available on your devices and to people you invite.

Contact form: your name, email, topic and message, used only to reply to you.

AI symptom checks

When you run an AI symptom check, the symptoms you selected, your pet's species, age and weight, your notes and any photo you attach are sent to our server and to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the result. They are not used to train AI models. We do not need your name for this.

Clinic and pharmacy partners

We share information with a vet clinic or pharmacy only when you connect it and ask us to, for example to import records, request an appointment or order a refill.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Synced records are protected so that only you and the people you invite can read them.

Your choices

You can export or delete your records at any time from the app, or ask us to delete your account by writing to connect@stormlabmarketing.com.

Children

CuteFamilyPet is meant for adults who care for pets. Children can use it with a parent's account.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email connect@stormlabmarketing.com or use our contact form.
